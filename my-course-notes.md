## Concepts which I think haven't been taught well

- did we introduce tools before we starting using them?
- Getting the AI to validate its work (not just looking for errors or hallicinations, but more like an editor - it can actually go back and read and evaluate, or even use Chrome)
- Interactive speccing for a document or a project works really well - go back and forth with the AI, getting it to answer your questions which leads to a good spec.  This REALLY needs a demo.
- AI isn't good at learning from its own mistakes - you need to proactively tell it to update context, skills etc
- PRACTICE is the only way you get good at working with AI.
- Remember, you the human are actually the bottleneck now, rather than the enabler
- A lot of the tips in this course are directly aimed at avoiding FRUSTRATION.  When you forget about context and prompts and skills and whatnot, the AI starts to act in frustrating ways.
- The failure of the AI is your failure, not the AIs failure
