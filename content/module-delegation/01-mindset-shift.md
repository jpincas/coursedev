# From Search Engine to Worker

This is the session that might make you uncomfortable. We're going to talk about what AI can actually do — and what that means for how you work.

## The Uncomfortable Truth

AI is already better than you at most routine knowledge tasks.

Not all tasks. But the bread and butter of knowledge work:
- Drafting documents
- Summarising information
- Analysing data
- Formatting reports
- Doing research

Faster. More thorough. More consistent.

This isn't a prediction about the future. This is now.

The question isn't whether to use AI. It's how to use it effectively.

## Two Mental Models

![Two Mental Models: AI as Search Engine vs AI as Worker](/content/module-delegation/images/mindset-shift.svg)

**AI as Search Engine**
Ask questions, get answers, copy-paste, repeat. You're doing all the assembly work.

**AI as Worker**
Delegate the outcome, step away, come back to finished work. AI does the assembly.
