# By End of Day, You'll Know How To:

Here's what you'll be able to do by the end of today. Not just "understand AI" in some abstract sense — practical capabilities.

1. **Understand why AI succeeds or fails at tasks** — You'll understand the mechanism well enough to predict when AI will struggle.

2. **Provide context that gets quality output** — You'll know how to set up tasks so they succeed.

3. **Delegate real work, not just ask questions** — You'll treat AI as a worker, not a search engine.

4. **Receive finished files, not chat responses** — You'll work in files and deliverables, not endless chat threads.

5. **Build repeatable AI-powered workflows** — You'll create systems, not just one-off prompts.

6. **Know when NOT to use AI** — Critically, you'll know when AI is the wrong tool for the job.
