# Key Takeaways

Let's summarize what we've learned about working with files.

## 1. Files, Not Chat

The deliverable is the point, not the conversation.

Chat is for communication. Files are for work. If you're copy-pasting from chat, you're doing it wrong.

## 2. Provide, Don't Describe

Upload your actual data. Don't summarise it in text.

AI works better with the real information than with your description of it. Eliminate the game of telephone.

## 3. Receive Deliverables

Real files you can use directly. Excel with working formulas. PowerPoint with real slides. Code that runs.

These are professional-quality deliverables, not chat text formatted to look like documents.

## 4. Ground with Specifics

Your style guide, your templates, your data, your examples.

AI produces generic output without grounding. Provide your specific materials to get output that fits your context.

```callout
type: tip
title: "The Mindset Shift"
content: "Think of AI as a team member who can create documents, spreadsheets, and presentations. You provide the inputs and requirements. They deliver the files. You review, iterate, and use."
```

## The Practical Test

For your next AI task, ask yourself:

- Am I copy-pasting from chat? → Request a file instead
- Am I describing my data? → Upload it instead
- Is the output too generic? → Provide grounding materials
- Am I reformatting constantly? → Request the right format upfront

## What This Enables

When you work in files:
- Tasks complete faster (no reformatting)
- Output is higher quality (proper functionality)
- Sharing is natural (send the file)
- Iteration is easy (edit the actual document)

This is how AI becomes genuinely useful for real work.
