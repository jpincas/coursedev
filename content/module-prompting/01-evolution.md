# From Prompting to Delegation

We've covered how LLMs work and why context matters. Now: how do you actually communicate with these systems to get what you want?

This isn't about magic words. It's about clear communication with a very capable but very literal system.

## The Rapid Evolution

![Evolution of Prompting: from Prompt Engineering to Delegation](/content/module-prompting/images/prompting-evolution.svg)

**2022: Prompt Engineering**
The early days. People obsessed over exact wording, magic phrases, specific formats. "Tricks" to get the model to behave.

**2024: Context Engineering**
The realisation that information matters more than clever wording. Providing the right context beats clever phrasing.

**2025: Delegation**
Specifying outcomes and letting AI figure out the how. Treating AI as a capable worker, not a text generator.

```callout
type: info
title: "Where We Are Now"
content: "We're past 'prompting' in the old sense. The models are good enough that you don't need tricks. You need clear communication about what you want and the context to achieve it."
```

## What This Means for You

You don't need to learn "prompting secrets." You need to learn clear communication:
- What do you actually want?
- What information does the AI need to produce it?
- What does success look like?

These are the same questions you'd ask when delegating to a human. The only difference is that AI needs more explicit context — it can't read your mind or infer from shared experience.
